# Act 3: The online store and deck building

**Planned.** Day 5 to 6 of play. The shared design is in
[story.md](story.md); this gathers what is planned for this act so far.

**Unlocked by:** summer, when Set 2 sells out everywhere and Gus mentions
PackDrop.com.

**Leads to:** [Act 4](act-4.md), when the first booster box is delivered.

## What's planned

- **PackDrop.com, the online store:** the cheapest packs in bulk, but they
  arrive at a set clock time. Standard shipping takes 4 hours, express 1
  hour for a fee, and slow free shipping 12 hours, arriving overnight. A
  package arriving while the player is away waits on the doorstep.
- **Booster boxes:** 24 packs for $60 ($2.50 a pack), with one guaranteed
  legendary-tier hit.
- **Preorders:** a new set has a release time, and preorders arrive then.
- **Automation, online only:** subscriptions and auto-preorders. Buying
  automatically at the vending machine would be scalping, so it's never
  allowed.
- **Building real decks,** and **regionals** against Riley (who mostly plays
  Water), every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00), one entry per block.
- **Flip's first offer:** buy out every store's stock of Set 2 and resell
  it (see Business offers in [story.md](story.md)).
