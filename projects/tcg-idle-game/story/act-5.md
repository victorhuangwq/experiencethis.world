# Act 5: Your own shop

**Planned.** Days 7 to 8 of play, and the first ending. The shared design
is in [story.md](story.md); this gathers what is planned for this act so
far.

**Unlocked by:** a money target, after the first card show in
[Act 4](act-4.md).

## What's planned

- **Quitting the day job:** the shop's income replaces the salary, and
  quitting is the moment that starts the act.
- **The shop:** stock the shelves, host tournaments, and refill the vending
  machine. The shop earns money while the player is away.
- **Pricing honestly:** price-gouging customers in your own shop is a
  strike.
- **Flip's second offer:** print counterfeits (see Business offers in
  [story.md](story.md)).
- **Set 3** is announced, then releases with the last legendaries.
- **The championship** against xX_K3V1N_Xx, with the champion deck (see
  Endings in [story.md](story.md)).

Act 5 closes the loop: the player ends up stocking the vending machine from
Act 1 (with the key from its coin return), and Pip is the first customer
through the door of the player's shop.
